How to review

  • Check each concept at Phone 390 first, in RTL, on both the Home and Player screens.
  • Switch to LTR to confirm the layout mirrors cleanly (progress bars, chevrons, durations at the end edge).
  • Then widen to Desktop. The toggles persist across concept files, so you can hop between them in the same mode.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts; everything else is inline. Thumbnails are placeholder gradients.
